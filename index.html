About Us

Shivam Steel Railing & Furniture fabricates and installs stainless steel railings, and also makes stainless steel furniture, from a workshop in Banashankari, Bengaluru.

The business is run by its proprietors, Ram Lal and Ramesh. Work starts with the opening: the length, the fixing, the finish, and whether the railing is for a home or a commercial building.

Services

Stainless Steel Railings

Fabricated and installed for houses, apartments, and commercial buildings.

Glass Railings

Glass for balcony edges and stairs where a clearer view is wanted.

Staircase Railings

Handrails and infill sized to the pitch and the fixing of that stair.

Balcony Railings

Sections made for balcony edges on homes and commercial buildings.

SS Furniture

Stainless steel furniture made alongside the railing work.

Custom SS Railings

Each railing is made to the measured opening, not a single catalogue size.
